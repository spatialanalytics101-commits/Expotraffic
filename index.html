The Mountaingate Lifestyle: What the Community Offers

Mountaingate is a well-established private residential community spanning around 870 acres at the crest of the Santa Monica Mountains, with approximately 300 homes across a series of distinct enclaves. The community operates with 24-hour guard-gated security; The Ridge maintains its own dedicated entry gate within that broader structure.

The Mountaingate Country Club — with 27 holes of golf across two courses, tennis courts, a spa, and a pro shop — is the recreational anchor of the community. Note that club membership is separate from HOA membership; prospective buyers should confirm availability directly. Beyond the club, the community features five pools, multiple clubhouses, and access to the Canyonback Trail and nearby routes through the Santa Monica Mountains.
