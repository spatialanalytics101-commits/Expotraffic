The Property: Exterior and Entry

The home is built in classic red brick with a hip slate roof and chimney, traditional in its proportions and well-settled into its site. A decorative iron gate with the street number in relief opens onto a herringbone brick walkway, which leads through manicured boxwood and mature trees to a substantial dark-wood double front door. An attached two-car garage integrates cleanly into the facade.

On the upper floor, a wrought-iron balcony is visible from the rear garden. The surrounding hedges and established canopy trees provide natural screening and contribute to the sense of enclosure within the property itself.

Inside: Foyer, Scale, and Natural Light

Entry is into a two-story circular foyer with a sweeping curved staircase, warm hardwood floors, and a wrought-iron chandelier suspended through the full height of the space. It is the kind of architectural gesture that sets a clear tone for the rest of the home.

The great room beyond rises to a double height, with floor-to-ceiling multi-pane windows pulling in natural light and framing a green garden view. A marble fireplace with a classical white mantle anchors one wall. French doors on the opposite side open to the garden terrace, creating a direct indoor-outdoor connection through the main living space.

The Kitchen

The kitchen is centered on a large cherry-wood island with an integrated gas cooktop and a professional stainless steel range hood. White perimeter cabinetry provides substantial storage; a bay window above the main sink looks out to the garden. A French door from the kitchen opens directly to the terrace, which works well for casual indoor-outdoor meals.
